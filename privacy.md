# Taro Chat Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 23, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Taro Chat Bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and processes information when you interact with it on Discord.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Process

When you interact with the Bot, the following information may be processed:

* Your Discord User ID.
* Messages and prompts that you send directly to the Bot.
* Conversation history used to maintain conversational context.
* Your selected AI model or other Bot preferences.
* Basic Discord information necessary for the Bot to receive and respond to messages.

The Bot does not intentionally request passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, or other sensitive credentials.

You should not send sensitive personal information to the Bot.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only as necessary to provide and operate the Bot.

This may include:

* Generating AI responses to your messages.
* Maintaining conversation context between messages.
* Remembering user-specific Bot preferences.
* Preventing abuse and troubleshooting technical problems.
* Improving the reliability and operation of the Bot.

## 3. AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Messages sent to the Bot may be transmitted to third-party services in order to generate AI responses.

Depending on the Bot's current configuration, these services may include:

* Discord, for message delivery and Bot functionality.
* Free Claude Code (FCC), used as a local AI routing and proxy service.
* AI model providers configured through FCC, such as NVIDIA NIM or other supported providers.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The specific AI model or provider used by the Bot may change over time.

## 4. Stored Conversation Data

The Bot may locally store limited conversation history associated with your Discord User ID so that it can remember recent context.

The Bot may also locally store user preferences such as the selected AI model.

This information is stored on infrastructure controlled by the Bot operator and is not intended to be publicly accessible.

Conversation history may be limited, replaced by newer messages, or deleted during maintenance or updates.

## 5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot and provide its features.

Conversation history may be automatically limited to a certain number of recent messages.

The Bot operator may periodically remove stored data.

## 6. Deleting Your Conversation Data

Users may clear conversation memory using the Bot's supported clear command, currently:

`!clear`

This removes the conversation history associated with the requesting user's Discord account from the Bot's active stored conversation memory.

Users may also contact the Bot owner or server administrator to request deletion of stored information associated with their Discord User ID.

## 7. Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to:

* Process AI requests through configured AI providers.
* Operate Discord functionality.
* Maintain or troubleshoot the Bot.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored Bot data and credentials.

However, no system or online service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid submitting confidential, highly sensitive, or security-critical information through the Bot.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements.

The Bot does not knowingly attempt to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or Discord policies.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality, storage methods, AI providers, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published at the same public location.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other concerns regarding Taro Chat Bot, contact the Bot owner or the administrator of the Discord server where the Bot is available.

## 12. Discord

Taro Chat Bot is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

Use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
